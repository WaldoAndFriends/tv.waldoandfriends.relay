Relay.fm brings you podcast episodes from the Relay.fm network.

This app provides global Flow tokens and a trigger to stay up-to-date with your favorite Relay.fm shows:

GLOBAL TOKENS
For every supported show, the app exposes three global tokens that always reflect the latest episode's title, media URL, and episode number. These tokens are available anywhere in your Flows — no action card needed. The values are loaded from each show's feed on startup and kept up-to-date by periodic polling.

TRIGGER: When a new episode is released
Triggers when any Relay.fm podcast publishes a new episode. Filter by a specific show or listen for all shows. Tokens include the episode title, URL, media URL, show name, episode number, subtitle, duration, and publish date.

CONFIGURATION
Visit the app settings to configure the polling interval (default: 15 minutes, minimum: 1 minute). You can also manually trigger a feed check from the settings page.

SHOWS
Supports all active Relay.fm shows: Analog(ue), BONANZA, B-Sides, Clockwise, Conduit, Connected, Cortex, Departures, Focused, Mac Power Users, Material, Paper Places, Reconcilable Differences, Roboism, The Backmarkers, The Pen Addict, Thoroughly Considered, and Upgrade.
